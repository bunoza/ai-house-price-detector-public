# AI House Price Detector

AI House Price Detector (shown on your device as "House Price AI") is an iOS app that uses AI to estimate property prices based on location, size, type, and features. Enter property details, get an AI-generated price range with a market analysis, and save reports on your device for future reference.

---

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 12, 2026**

### Overview

AI House Price Detector ("the App", "we", "us") is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not require an account, does not collect personally identifiable information, and does not track you. Your reports are stored only on your device.

### Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information.
- We do not access your device's GPS or location services.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
- We do not operate our own servers, so we do not receive or store your property details, photos, or reports.

### Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is created by you and stored **only on your device** using Apple's SwiftData framework:

- Property details you enter: city, state/region/country, neighborhood, property type, size, lot size, year built, stories, bedrooms, bathrooms, floor level, parking and garage, amenities, HOA details, condition, renovation status, and any notes you add
- An optional property photo you choose to attach to a report (stored as a reduced-size copy)
- AI-generated price estimates, full analysis reports, and market insights
- Whether a report is included in your portfolio totals

The following settings are stored on your device using Apple's `UserDefaults`:

- Your unit system preference (Imperial or Metric)
- Your currency preference (or automatic detection)
- Whether you have agreed to share property details with OpenAI

**Currency auto-detection** works only from the country or region you type into the Location field. It does not use your device's location.

Data stored on your device may be included in your device or iCloud backups, depending on your own backup settings. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by the App.

### Your Consent Before Any Data Is Sent

Before your first analysis, the App asks for your permission to send property details to OpenAI. Nothing is sent until you tap **Agree & Continue**. You can withdraw this permission at any time in **Settings → Privacy** by turning off "Share property data with OpenAI". Analyses cannot be generated without this permission.

### Information Sent to Third-Party Services

**OpenAI (for price analysis)**
When you request an analysis, the following is sent to OpenAI's API over an encrypted HTTPS connection:

- The city, state/region/country, and neighborhood you entered
- The property details you entered (such as type, size, rooms, year built, amenities, HOA, condition, and renovation status)
- Any notes you typed in the notes field
- Your selected unit system and currency

**Property photos are never sent to OpenAI.** No name, contact details, account information, or device identifiers are included in the request.

Please do not include personal information (such as names, phone numbers, or exact street addresses) in the notes field, because its contents are sent to OpenAI as written.

OpenAI states that data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default and may be retained for a limited period for abuse and misuse monitoring. See [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) and [OpenAI's API data usage policies](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy) for details.

**RevenueCat (for subscription management)**
The App uses RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription, to show subscription options, and to process purchases and restores through the Apple App Store. RevenueCat assigns an anonymous App User ID (visible in **Settings**) and may process device information and purchase history. We do not receive your payment details. All payments are handled by Apple. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

**Apple (MapKit and App Store)**
City suggestions in the Location field are powered by Apple's MapKit. Apple may process the characters you type to return suggestions. Subscriptions are purchased and billed through the Apple App Store. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Photos

If you attach a property photo, you pick it using Apple's system photo picker. The App only receives the specific photo you select, not your photo library, and does not request photo library permission. The photo is stored with your report on your device and is never uploaded or sent to any third party.

### PDF Reports

If you export a report as a PDF, the file is generated on your device and shared through the iOS share sheet to the destination you choose (for example Messages, Mail, or Files). Once you share it, the recipient or service you selected handles it under their own policies.

### Data Retention and Deletion

- **Reports:** kept on your device until you delete them. You can delete any report from the Saved tab. Uninstalling the App removes all reports, photos, and settings stored by the App.
- **OpenAI:** analysis requests are handled under OpenAI's retention policy described above.
- **RevenueCat and Apple:** subscription records are kept by RevenueCat and Apple as needed to provide and verify your subscription.

### Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data and to withdraw consent.

- Data on your device is fully under your control and can be deleted at any time as described above.
- You can withdraw consent to sharing property details with OpenAI in **Settings → Privacy**.
- To request deletion of subscription records held by RevenueCat, contact us and include the App User ID shown in **Settings**.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on your **consent** to send property details to OpenAI, and on **performance of a contract** to manage your subscription.

### Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.

### Contact
