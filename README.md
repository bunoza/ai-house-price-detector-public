# AI House Price Detector

AI House Price Detector is an iOS app that uses AI to estimate property prices based on location, size, type, and features. Enter property details, get an instant AI-generated price range, and save reports for future reference.

---

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 16, 2026**

### Overview

AI House Price Detector ("the App") is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information.

### Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
- We do not collect precise GPS location or device location data.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not share your data with advertisers.

### Information Processed Locally

The following data is entered by you and stored **only on your device** using Apple's SwiftData framework:

- Property details you enter (city, state/country, neighborhood, property type, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, amenities, condition)
- AI-generated price estimates and full analysis reports
- Your unit system preference (Imperial or Metric) and currency setting

This data never leaves your device except as described below.

### Information Sent to Third-Party Services

**OpenAI (for price analysis)**
When you request a property analysis, the property details you entered are sent to OpenAI's API to generate a price estimate. This data is transmitted securely over HTTPS. Please refer to [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) for how OpenAI handles API request data.

**RevenueCat (for subscription management)**
If you purchase a premium subscription or restore purchases, the App communicates with RevenueCat to verify your entitlement. RevenueCat may collect device identifiers and purchase information as described in [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

**Apple MapKit (for location suggestions)**
City and address autocomplete suggestions are powered by Apple's MapKit framework. Apple may process the characters you type to return location suggestions. Refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details.

### Free Tier Usage Tracking

The App tracks the number of free analyses you have used on your device using Apple's `UserDefaults`. This count (a single integer) is stored locally and is not transmitted to any server.

### Data Retention and Deletion

All analysis history is stored locally on your device. You can delete individual reports or all history at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

### Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

### Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the App Store support link.
